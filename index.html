Can you text as well as our participants?

In our study, midlife adults copied words on their phones while walking a 6 m course. Try the same 30-second texting task and see how you compare.

How it works

  1. Tap Start to see a list of 25 five-letter words.
  2. Type the words in order, separated by spaces. The 30-second timer starts with your first letter.
  3. We score it the way the study did: words typed correctly and in order.

For the full experience, walk on the spot while you type.
